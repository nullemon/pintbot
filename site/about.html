About Tokyo Copyright

We build and operate the technical infrastructure that rights holders use to find and remove pirated copies of their work — anywhere in the world.

Who we are

A technical firm, not a law firm

Tokyo Copyright was founded on a simple observation: the bottleneck in anti-piracy is not the law, it is throughput. A single popular title can be re-uploaded thousands of times a week across dozens of platforms. No legal team can keep up with that by hand.

So we approached it as an engineering problem. Our crawlers, fingerprint matching and notice pipeline run continuously, and our people concentrate on the cases that genuinely need human judgement — ambiguous matches, repeat offenders, hostile hosts and pre-release leaks.

We work alongside our clients' legal counsel; we do not replace them and we do not provide legal advice.

Principles

How we choose to work

These are commitments, not marketing lines. They constrain what we will accept as an assignment.

Never against end users

We do not sue, invoice, threaten or send demand letters to viewers, readers or listeners. Our work targets the distribution of infringing copies, not the audience.

Accuracy before volume

A wrong takedown removes lawful speech and damages the client. Confidence thresholds, allow-lists for licensees, and human review on borderline cases are built into the pipeline.

Proportionate response

We start with the host or platform that can resolve the issue directly, and escalate to registrars, payment providers or search engines only when that fails.

Verified rights only

Before a single notice is sent we confirm that the client actually holds the rights they claim, for the territories they claim.

Transparent to the other side

Every notice identifies us, states the legal basis, and explains how to file a counter-notice if the recipient disagrees.

Data minimisation

We collect the evidence needed to prove an infringement and no more. We do not build profiles of individual internet users.

Approach

What working with us looks like

Most engagements begin with a piracy assessment: you give us a handful of titles, we report back on where they are currently being distributed illegally and at what scale. That report is yours whether or not you continue.

If you do continue, onboarding takes a few days. We ingest your catalogue and proof of rights, generate reference fingerprints, register your release calendar so that new episodes are protected from the minute they go live, and set up your dashboard and API credentials.

From there the system runs by itself. You receive scheduled reporting, a live view of detections and removals, and a named contact in Tokyo for anything that needs a person.

Please note: Tokyo Copyright provides technical enforcement services. We are not a law firm and nothing on this website constitutes legal advice. Where litigation is appropriate, we support your counsel with evidence and reporting.
